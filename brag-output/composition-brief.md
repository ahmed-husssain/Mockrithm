Create a short, premium product launch video for Mockrithm that introduces the platform, showcases the AI mock interview experience, and drives candidates to start practicing.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Sleek, authoritative, high-craft engineering launch film
- Interpretation: Ultra-clean contrast, smooth linear easing, purposeful typography, zero generic SaaS stock cliches.
- Angle: From interview anxiety to FAANG readiness through focused telemetry-guided practice.
- Hook: 0s-3.5s — Dark cosmic canvas, pulsating audio waveform, and the opening truth: "Top tech interviews aren't won on luck. They're mastered in the sandbox."
- Outro: 17.5s-20s — Pristine brand signature, glowing logo mark, and "Begin Journey → mockrithm.com".
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language ("supercharge your workflow")
  - Abstract spinning shapes or 3D clutter
  - Cluttered screens or unreadable flashing text

## Storyboard
Use the storyboard in `brag-output/brag-plan.md` as the creative contract:
1. **Scene 1 (0s–3.5s)**: The Hook — Cosmic space, pulsating waveform, opening claim.
2. **Scene 2 (3.5s–8.5s)**: The Conversational AI Engine — Live audio telemetry (135 WPM, 0 filler words, 94% STAR score).
3. **Scene 3 (8.5s–14.0s)**: The Coding Sandbox — IDE window, test assertions passing, $O(N)$ complexity badge.
4. **Scene 4 (14.0s–17.5s)**: Readiness Scorecard — 96% readiness score gauge animation and telemetry badges.
5. **Scene 5 (17.5s–20.0s)**: Outro & Call to Action — Mockrithm brand logo, tagline, and Begin Journey button.
